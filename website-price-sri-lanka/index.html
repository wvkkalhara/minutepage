Website cost questions, answered

How much does a website cost in Sri Lanka?

With MinutePage, a 1–3 page website starts at Rs. 29,000 (Starter Launch), a full business website with up to 8 pages is Rs. 55,000 (Business Growth), and an online store or booking system is Rs. 95,000 (Commerce & Systems Pro). Every package includes a free .lk or .com domain, SSL and hosting for the first year.

Are there monthly fees?

No monthly hosting fees. From year two you can renew your domain, SSL, monitoring and priority updates with Annual Care & Cloud Protection for Rs. 12,000 ($49) a year. Payment-gateway fees, if you take card payments, are charged by those providers.

How much does an online store cost in Sri Lanka?

Commerce & Systems Pro is Rs. 95,000 ($599). It includes PayHere, Onepay, Stripe or cash on delivery, an admin stock dashboard and automated WhatsApp order alerts, live in 7 to 10 days.

Do I need a business registration (BR)?

No. Your name and a WhatsApp number are enough to start. Some card gateways ask for a BR during their own checks, and we guide you through that if needed.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer, in Sri Lanka or internationally. You pay 50% to start and 50% when your site is ready to launch. We never ask for card details on our website.

Who owns and manages the domain and website?

MinutePage registers the domain and keeps the website files and access, and we look after the hosting, SSL, security and renewals for you, so there's nothing technical for you to handle. The text, photos and logo you give us stay yours.
