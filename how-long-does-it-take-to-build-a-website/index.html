How long does it take to build a website?

A small business website can be live in a few days. Many take months. The difference is almost never the building: it is the waiting.

The honest answer

The work of designing and building a simple business site is a few days. What stretches a project to weeks or months is waiting for text, photos, decisions and replies. If your content is ready on day one, the site can be too.

Typical timelines

These are the timelines we work to at MinutePage, counted from the day we have your content and your advance.

  • 1 to 3 pages for a shop, service or class: about 3 days
  • A full business site with up to 8 pages: about 5 days
  • An online store or booking system: 7 to 10 days
  • A custom web app or internal system: scoped case by case

What actually takes the time

On most projects the build is the shortest part.

  • Content: writing about the business, listing services and prices
  • Photos: finding or taking pictures that are good enough to use
  • Decisions: several people need to agree on the look or the wording
  • Accounts: a payment gateway or business email waiting for approval
  • Feedback: changes sent a few at a time over many days

How to get yours live faster

You can remove most of the delay before the project starts.

  • Collect your logo, photos, services and prices in one WhatsApp message or folder
  • Choose one person who makes the final decisions
  • Send all your feedback on the preview in one go
  • Launch with the pages you need, then add more later
  • Start applications such as payment gateways early, since approval is outside anyone’s control

What you can see while it is being built

You should never have to wonder what is happening. Ask any designer how you will follow progress. At MinutePage every project gets a tracking ID and a private preview link within 48 hours, so you can open the real site on your own phone long before it is finished.

After launch

Going live is not the end. Google takes anywhere from a few days to a few weeks to list a new site, and it climbs as people visit it and as your Google Business Profile gathers reviews. Plan to look at it again after a month and adjust.

Questions, answered

Can a website really be built in 3 days?

Yes, for a 1 to 3 page site, when the text and photos are ready at the start. The build is quick; collecting content is what usually takes time.

Why do some agencies take months?

Larger teams, more meetings and waiting for content. For a big custom system that time is real work. For a small business site it is mostly waiting.

How soon will my new site appear on Google?

Usually within days to a few weeks of launch. Submitting it in Google Search Console and linking it from your Google Business Profile helps.
