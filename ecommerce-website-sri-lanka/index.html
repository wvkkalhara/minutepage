Questions, answered

How much does an online store cost in Sri Lanka?

Commerce & Systems Pro is Rs. 95,000 ($599), one-time. It includes your online store or booking system, payments, a stock and order dashboard, WhatsApp order alerts, and your domain, SSL and hosting for the first year.

Which payment methods can my store accept?

PayHere, Onepay, Stripe or cash on delivery. Card gateways run their own merchant checks, and some ask for a Business Registration (BR). We guide you through that.

How long does it take?

Usually 7 to 10 days. You get a private preview link within 48 hours of your advance, and you can follow every step in the live project tracker.

Can I start without a business registration?

Yes. Cash on delivery and WhatsApp ordering work without a BR. A BR is only needed if a card gateway asks for one.

Are there monthly fees?

No monthly hosting fees. From year two, Annual Care & Cloud Protection (Rs. 12,000 / $49 a year) renews your domain, SSL and hosting. Payment gateways charge their own transaction fees.
