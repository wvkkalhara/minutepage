# MinutePage reels: how to finish them in CapCut

Three vertical reels, 1080×1920, 16 seconds, 30 fps. No voice and no music inside, so you can add both in CapCut.

| `reel-2-before-after.mp4` | EnerFab: old efpl.org loads in 6.46s, new enerfab.lk in 1.12s |
| `reel-3-online-store.mp4` | Online shop for Rs. 35,000, no payment gateway, orders to WhatsApp |
| `reel-4-what-you-get.mp4` | **24 s.** What each package includes: Launch Page, Starter, Online Store, Business Growth, then what every package gets, then WhatsApp |
| `beat-guide-120bpm.wav` | 24 s of clicks, one on every beat (high click = start of each bar). Only for checking timing; don't publish it |

## The beat

Everything is timed to **120 BPM**: one beat every 0.5 s, one bar (4 beats) every 2 s.

- Every new item appears exactly on a beat.
- Bigger changes happen at the start of a bar: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 14 s.
- The WhatsApp number appears at **12.0 s** in reels 1–3, and at **20.0 s** in reel 4. Make that your music's strongest hit or drop.
- Reel 4 changes package every 4 s, at 2, 6, 10 and 14 s.

## Adding music in CapCut (desktop)

1. Open CapCut, start a new project and import the reel.
2. Drag the reel onto the timeline. If CapCut asks, keep the 9:16 ratio.
3. Open **Audio → Music** and choose a track around **120 BPM**. Upbeat, corporate, tech and "lo-fi house" tracks are often close to that; the track's details sometimes show the BPM.
4. Turn on CapCut's **Beat** markers for the music (right-click the music clip, or use the beat button above the timeline, depending on your version).
5. Slide the music so a beat marker sits exactly at **0:00**, and check that markers also fall at 2.0, 4.0 and 12.0 s. Those are where the cuts are.
6. If you can't tell, import `beat-guide-120bpm.wav` as a second audio track, line the music's beats up with its clicks, then delete the click track.
7. Lower the music to about 30–40% if you add your voice.
8. Export at 1080p, 30 fps.

Menu names differ between your CapCut versions (1.5 and 9.8). If something isn't where these steps say, look for the same word in the right-hand panel.

## Recording your voice (later)

Record on your phone in a quiet room, holding it about one hand-width from your mouth. Each line below fits its time slot when said at a normal pace. In CapCut, use **Record** (the microphone icon) or import the recordings, and line each one up with its start time.
